About me

A designer who builds, a builder who sketches first, and a firm believer in the five-person usability test.

my story

I started as a front-end developer who kept redrawing the designs I was handed. Eventually I just started drawing them first.

Twelve years on, I split my time between interface design and the code that ships it. Working both sides means fewer hand-offs, fewer "that is not what I drew" conversations and a faster route from idea to something people can click.

I work with founders, small agencies and in-house teams, usually on one project at a time. When I am not in Figma you will find me on a bike, in a bakery, or watching a stranger try to use something I made without saying a word.

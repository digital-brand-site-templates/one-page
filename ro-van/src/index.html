About

i am a graphic & web designer

I'm Ro-van, an independent designer with eleven years of making brands look as good as they work. I take a project from the first sketch to the final file: the logo and type system, the website that carries it, and the photos and short films that launch it.

Clients come to me because one person holds the whole picture. That means fewer hand-offs, a brand that feels the same on a coffee box as it does on a phone, and a designer who answers every email personally.

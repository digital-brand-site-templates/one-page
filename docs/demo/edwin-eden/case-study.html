Fieldnote: notes for site engineers

A voice-first notes app for engineers who write their reports with gloves on.

role
UX / UI design and front-end build
timeline
Seven weeks, one demo every Friday
tools
Figma, HTML, CSS
client
Fieldnote Ltd, a civil engineering consultancy

the brief

Fieldnote's engineers spent their evenings retyping site notes from scraps of paper. The company wanted a phone app that captured a note in the field and filed it against the right job without a second pass at a desk.

what I found

I spent two days on site and one in the office. Three things kept coming up:

  • Gloves, rain and sunlight made small tap targets useless.
  • Notes were only useful when they carried the job number and a photo.
  • Nobody opened an app that needed a login before the first note.

what we built

One big record button, dictation that tags the job from a short code, and a photo that attaches itself to the note you are writing. Login happens after the first note, never before it.

The interface uses a single type size above 20px and a 56px minimum tap target, which tested well with work gloves on.

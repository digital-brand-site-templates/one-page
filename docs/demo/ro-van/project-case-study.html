Kiln Roasters

A new identity, coffee packaging and a 40-second launch film for a small roastery in Porto, made in one six-week project.

Clip info

Client
Kiln Roasters, Porto
My role
Identity, packaging, art direction, photo and film
Timeline
Six weeks, spring 2026
Delivered
Logo and type system, three box designs, brand guide, launch film
Tools
Illustrator, Photoshop, Premiere Pro

The brief

Kiln roast in small batches in a former pottery on the edge of Porto. Their coffee was winning tastings, but their bags looked like every other roaster's: kraft paper, a round logo and a printed label for the origin. Cafés kept their beans and hid the bag under the counter.

They wanted a box that a café would leave on the shelf on purpose, a look that could stretch to cups and a website, and something to show at the launch of their new espresso blend.

What I did

The name came from the building, so the identity did too. I spent a day at the roastery photographing the old kiln doors, the glaze colours left on the bricks and the roast curves on the screen.

  • A wordmark cut from the kiln's arched door, with a compact mark for cups and lids
  • Three box colours taken from the glazes, one per roast, with the tasting notes printed in gold
  • A one-page brand guide with the colours, type and photo style, written for the café staff
  • A 40-second launch film shot in the roastery and cut for Instagram and the website

The result

  • +18new café stockists in three months
  • 2.4xonline bean sales in the launch month
  • 96Kviews of the launch film in its first week
